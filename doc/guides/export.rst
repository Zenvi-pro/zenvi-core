Export and Delivery
===================

This guide covers settings that help preserve visual quality while keeping
render time practical.

Preset selection
----------------

1. Start with an output preset that matches your target platform.
2. Keep frame rate aligned with source footage whenever possible.
3. Use hardware encoding only after confirming quality on a short preview.

.. warning::

   Extremely high bitrate settings can make uploads slow without visible quality
   gains. Test on a representative section before full export.

Recommended baseline
--------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 35 35

   * - Target
     - Resolution / FPS
     - Suggested quality
   * - YouTube long-form
     - 1080p, source FPS
     - High quality preset
   * - Reels / Shorts
     - 1080x1920, source FPS
     - Medium-high quality preset
   * - Review draft
     - 720p, 24/30 FPS
     - Medium preset for faster turnaround
