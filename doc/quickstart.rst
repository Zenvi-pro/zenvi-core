Quickstart
==========

Get from empty project to export in minutes.

1. Create a new project and choose a profile that matches your source footage.
2. Import clips into **Project Files** and drag them into timeline tracks.
3. Trim clips to remove dead space and add transitions where needed.
4. Review audio levels and enable captions if your platform supports them.
5. Export with a preset matching your delivery platform.

.. tip::

   Export a short 20-second segment first when validating new presets.
